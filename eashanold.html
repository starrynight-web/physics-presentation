Brewster's Law

n = tan θB

At Brewster's angle, reflected light is 100% polarized ⊥ to the plane of incidence.

What Happens?

  • When unpolarized light hits a surface at Brewster's angle, the reflected ray becomes completely plane-polarized.
  • Reflected and refracted rays are perpendicular (90°).
  • Brewster angle depends only on refractive index: θ_B = arctan(n)
  • Used in polarizers, laser cavities, anti-glare coatings, photography filters.

Real-World Examples

Polarized sunglasses block horizontally polarized light (glare from roads/water). They work best when you tilt your head ~55–60° — close to Brewster's angle for glass/water!
